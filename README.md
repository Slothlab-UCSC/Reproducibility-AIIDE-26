# On the Reproducibility of Technical Game AI Research

This repository contains our reproductions of papers we performed as case studies for our work *On the Reproducibility of Technical Game AI Research* to be published at [AIIDE 2026](https://sites.google.com/view/aiide2026/home)

## Case Studies 

- [Playing Rollercoaster Tycoon](rct/) based on Campbell, J., & Verbrugge, C. (2025, April). *Playing RollerCoaster Tycoon with Reinforcement Learning*. In Proceedings of the 20th International Conference on the Foundations of Digital Games
- [Generating Connections Puzzles](connections/) based on Merino, T., Earle, S., Sudhakaran, R., Sudhakaran, S., & Togelius, J. (2024, November). *Making new connections: LLMs as puzzle generators for the New York times' connections word game*. In Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Interactive Digital Entertainment
- [Playing Slay the Spire](playing_sts/) based on Bateni, B., & Whitehead, J. (2024, May). *Language-driven play: Large language models as game-playing agents in Slay the Spire*. In Proceedings of the 19th International Conference on the Foundations of Digital Games
- [Generating Cards](card_generation/) based on Chen, T., & Guy, S. (2020, October). *Chaos cards: Creating novel digital card games through grammatical content generation and meta-based card evaluation*. In Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Interactive Digital Entertainment
- [Playing Codenames](codenames/) based on Sidji, M., & Stephenson, M. (2024, August). *Prompt engineering ChatGPT for Codenames*. In 2024 IEEE Conference on Games
- [Opinionated Virtual Characters](lyra/) based on Azad, S., & Martens, C. (2019, October). *Lyra: Simulating believable opinionated virtual characters*. In Proceedings of the AAAI conference on artificial intelligence and interactive digital entertainment
- [Story Quality](story_quality/) based on Behrooz, M., Robertson, J., & Jhala, A. (2019, October). *Story quality as a matter of perception: using word embeddings to estimate cognitive interest*. In Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Interactive Digital Entertainment
- [Playing Pandemic](pandemic/) based on Sauma-Chacón, P., & Eger, M. (2020, October). *PAIndemic: A planning agent for Pandemic*. In Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Interactive Digital Entertainment
